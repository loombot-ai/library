## Why not one licence everywhere

The entries are advice, and advice is worth more when it travels; CC BY asks only that the reader knows where it
came from. The scripts are programs, and a program under a copyleft licence cannot be dropped into somebody's
tooling without a discussion about their whole codebase — which is a conversation that stops people using it.

loom itself is a different thing and a different licence; this repository is not loom's source.
